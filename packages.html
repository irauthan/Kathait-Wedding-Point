Booking Terms & Policies

We maintain simple, honest commercial agreements with clear guidelines so your family experiences zero surprises on the big day.

  • Date Blocking Advance: A 25% non-refundable deposit blocks your chosen dates on our official calendar.
  • Final Balance: Remaining balance is due 10 days prior to the event check-in date.
  • Date Rescheduling: In unavoidable family situations, dates can be moved subject to availability if notified 30 days prior.
  • Outside Caterers: We welcome outside caterers and provide dedicated kitchen and utility spaces with water supply.
