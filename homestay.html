Prime Location

Strategically Located Near Chandrabadni Temple

Situated on the main highway in Jamnikhal, Kathait Homestay offers the most accessible and peaceful hotel accommodation for visitors to Chandrabadni and Devprayag.

Maa Chandrabadni Temple, one of the sacred Shakti Peethas perched on the Chandrakut mountain peak, is easily reachable from our homestay via a scenic drive. Pilgrims and tourists love staying here to enjoy morning mountain breezes, home-cooked food, and peaceful rest.

Chandrabadni Temple Proximity

Short scenic drive to the base of the Chandrabadni approach road.

Devprayag Sangam

Around 15-20 minutes drive to the holy confluence of Alaknanda & Bhagirathi rivers.

Secure Highway Parking

Direct roadside accessibility with spacious on-site vehicle parking.
