Effortless Travel

How to Reach Us

Situated on the main arterial highway in Jamnikhal, reaching Kathait Wedding Point & Homestay is convenient by personal car, taxi, or state buses.

From Rishikesh

Approx. 75 km via NH-58 Badrinath Highway through Devprayag Sangam. Smooth 2 to 2.5 hour scenic mountain drive.

From Dehradun Airport

Jolly Grant Airport is approx. 95 km away. Taxis and rental cars are easily available directly to Jamnikhal.

From Haridwar

Approx. 100 km. Regular Uttarakhand roadways buses and shared cabs run towards Devprayag and Tehri routes daily.

To Chandrabadni Temple

Just 15-20 minutes drive uphill from our complex in Jamnikhal to the holy Shakti Peeth pilgrimage shrine.
