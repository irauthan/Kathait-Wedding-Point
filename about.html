Introducing Kathait Wedding Point & Homestay

Located in Jamnikhal, Devprayag near Chandrabadni, Uttarakhand, we are the ultimate destination where luxury hospitality meets natural Himalayan beauty.

Kathait Wedding Point & Homestay is strategically situated in Jamnikhal near the holy town of Devprayag and the revered Chandrabadni route. Surrounded by majestic Garhwal mountains, our venue provides a dreamy, serene backdrop for your most cherished life celebrations and peaceful hotel stays.

Whether planning a grand wedding, corporate conference, seeking a peaceful retreat in our luxury homestay rooms near Chandrabadni, or maintaining your health routine at our state-of-the-art gym, we guarantee exceptional service, elegant atmosphere, and traditional hospitality.
